About the game

Diamond League

Rules

Everyone fights everyone on one arena. In last standing mode you have one life, and a liquid rises from below and pushes everyone to the top. In deathmatch mode you come back after two seconds, and the first to ten kills wins.

Recoil is your second jump

Every shot pushes you away from the barrel. Aim down and shoot to fly higher than a jump can take you. The heavier the gun, the stronger the kick.

Crates and blocks

Crates protect you from fire. When broken, they drop a weapon, a medkit, a shield or a crystal. Crystals are the only source of blocks — blocks do not refill on their own. Use blocks to build cover, or steps where you cannot jump.

Tall grass

If you stand in tall grass, enemies cannot see you. But the sound of a shot gives away your position for one second.

Weapons

  • Pistol — starting gun, unlimited ammo.
  • SMG — fast fire, weak kick, good for flying.
  • Shotgun — seven pellets and strong recoil, best up close.
  • Sniper — one precise shot at long range.
  • Launcher — the blast hurts everyone nearby.
  • Ricochet — bullets bounce three times and hit harder after each bounce.
  • Piercer — goes through players and walls, and the kick launches you like a rocket.
